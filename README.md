One monologue of about 100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is
secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm
and the small deflections — chamomile tea, house rules for nemeses, a
cat with seniority, tax ledgers in the blue room — never in a plain
statement that he is weary. He is still a villain: Castle Vorgath, the
Year of the Weeping Toad, a kingdom he is happy to hand over because
the plumbing has not worked since. The program writes the monologue to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
